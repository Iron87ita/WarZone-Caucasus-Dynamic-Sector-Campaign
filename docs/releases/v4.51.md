# WarZone Caucasus v4.51 – Reactive CAP and Campaign Briefing

## Flight briefing

- **Reactive CAP.** An offensive player or AI mission, or a detected attack on a ground group or logistics site, can alert the defending coalition. A pair of fighters starts hot at the nearest eligible friendly airfield with suitable parking. If the closest ramp is unavailable, the controller tries the next field. Track the response in **F10 Other → 02 WarZone | Coalition → 02 Intelligence → Quick reaction CAP status**.
- **Airspace and fleet.** BARCAP uses the revised Central Air Corridor. Regular CAP covers assigned friendly sectors. The NATO carrier group, its escort and its southern support zones have moved. A BLUE Su-27 client slot at Sukhumi starts with engines running.
- **Aircraft and support.** The RF dispatch templates use MiG-29S aircraft for western CAP, JF-17s for eastern CAP and two Su-30s for SEAD. NATO SEAD uses the F-16C. RF tanker and AWACS routes, ground group compositions and mission weather have been revised. Supply, capture and map-marker deliveries use a route speed of 155 m/s (about 301 kt).
- **F10 menus.** Open **Flight → Missions** for sector tasks, friendly AI escorts and enemy AI intercepts. Sensors, wallet and shop, logistics, and deployment orders have their own flight menus. Coalition reports, intelligence, air support and shared logistics sit under **Coalition**.
- **Personal credits.** Eligible kills and completed player tasks include 10 additional credits. Sector completion pays 60 for CAP, 70 for BARCAP, 65 for CAS, 55 for SEAD and 30 for STRIKE. Each credited strategic STRIKE object pays 20. Land at a friendly recovery point and wait for the 10-second confirmation to collect pending awards. Manual supply deliveries and rescued-pilot drop-offs pay their personal bonuses directly.
